## r/LucidDreaming

**Title:** I made Dreamfeather, an Android app for a sleep log, dream journal, and WBTB + MILD practice

**Summary:** A transparent, discussion-first introduction to my lucid dreaming app, with an invitation for feedback.

**Text:**

Hi everyone — I’m Carlos, and I made Dreamfeather, an Android app for practicing lucid dreaming. It has a sleep log, a dream journal with recurring dream-sign patterns, a WBTB alarm, and guided MILD and SSILD. The WBTB + MILD approach is grounded in research, but outcomes vary; the app is a practice aid, not a promise of lucid dreams or a medical tool.

It’s free for 14 days, then the core sleep log and journal stay free. Pro is a one-time $9.99 USD purchase (local pricing elsewhere). Android 9+. Sleep and journal records are stored on the phone; Google Play purchases, optional speech services, exports and Health Connect have their own data flows described in the privacy policy.

I built it because I wanted a structured way to keep a sleep log and practice consistently. If this kind of tool is useful to you, it’s on Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=eu.forextradingtools.dreamfeather

I’m happy to answer questions about how the app works or what I learned building it. What do you use to keep track of dream signs or practice MILD?
