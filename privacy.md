# Dreamfeather privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Dreamfeather is made by Carlos Oliveira, [hello@dreamfeather.app](mailto:hello@dreamfeather.app). This policy covers the Dreamfeather Android app.

## The short version

Your sleep log and dream journal are stored in the app's private storage on your phone, not on a developer-operated server. There is no Dreamfeather account, advertising, analytics SDK or tracking SDK. Google Play handles purchases. Optional speech providers, Health Connect and exports are described below.

## What the app stores, and where

The app stores the following **only on your phone**, in its private storage:

- sleep sessions you record (start and end times, pauses, quality, notes, technique),
- dream journal entries (text, feelings, tags, recall, lucid and nightmare flags),
- reality-check history, alarm and app settings, the trial start date and the last purchase status confirmed by Google Play,
- Health Connect record identifiers, source app package names and sync times when you import or export sleep records.

Dreamfeather does not upload your sleep log or journal to the developer. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this app. User-chosen exports and optional services are exceptions to local-only processing, as explained below.

## When data leaves your phone

The following features use other apps or services:

- **Backup and journal export.** When you export a backup or your journal, the app writes a file to the place you pick. That can be a local folder or a cloud-storage provider. Exported files are not encrypted by Dreamfeather. Protect them yourself and manage or delete them at the destination.
- **Health Connect.** If you grant access, Dreamfeather reads sleep sessions and stages when you tap Import, and writes completed sessions and their awake intervals when you finish a night. It does not request other health data or send Health Connect records to the developer. Other apps with your permission may read the records you export to Health Connect. You can revoke access and delete those records in Health Connect. Dreamfeather's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the [Health Connect Permissions policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12991134), including the Limited Use requirements.
- **Speech.** Dictating a dream opens an installed speech recognition app, which handles microphone access and returns recognized text. Read-aloud guidance and night cues pass text to your selected text-to-speech engine. This can include your dream signs or a custom cue phrase. Depending on the provider, voice and settings, audio or text may be processed online under that provider's privacy terms. Dreamfeather does not record or store microphone audio. Typing, sleep logging and alarms do not require speech services.
- **Feedback and support.** If you choose Report a bug or Suggest an idea, Dreamfeather opens your email app with an editable message that includes the app version, Android version and device manufacturer/model. Nothing is sent until you send the email. The developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and uses them to respond and investigate your request. Journal entries are not attached automatically. To request deletion of support correspondence, contact [hello@dreamfeather.app](mailto:hello@dreamfeather.app). Records needed to meet legal obligations may be retained.

## Purchases

The optional lifetime unlock is sold through Google Play under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google processes your payment and maintains the purchase record. Dreamfeather uses the Google Play Billing library to query product details, start a purchase, check ownership and acknowledge completed purchases using a purchase token. The app receives purchase information from Play, but never your card or bank details.

Dreamfeather saves only the last confirmed ownership status locally so Pro remains available offline. It does not send purchase tokens to a developer-operated server. Google Play purchase and ownership checks require connectivity; a Google account may be required by Play, not by Dreamfeather. Play Console declares purchase history as collected for app functionality, optional and not shared with third parties. Purchases are not used by Dreamfeather for advertising or behavioral analytics.

## Permissions

Notifications (alarms, reality checks, the morning reminder), exact alarms and full-screen alarms (so alarms ring on time), and Health Connect sleep access (only if you connect it). Fingerprint or face unlock is handled entirely by Android when you turn on the app lock; the app never sees biometric data.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes your local sleep sessions, dream entries, tags and reality-check history and resets app settings. It retains the trial start date and last confirmed purchase status, so deletion does not restart the trial or remove your unlock. Uninstalling removes the app's private local data.

Neither action deletes files you exported, Health Connect records, support emails or Google's purchase records. Manage exported files at their destination, Health Connect records in Health Connect, support correspondence through the contact above, and Google records through Google's controls. Reinstalling can restore your unlock from Google Play.

## Children

Dreamfeather is intended for users aged 13 and over, including teenagers. It is not directed at children under 13.
